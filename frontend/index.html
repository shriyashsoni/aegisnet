How it works

Four phases, one request.

An agent asks to act. AegisNet checks who it is, builds the route, decides if a human must sign off, then settles it onchain.

  1. Plan (0G Compute)

    The AI agent plans execution parameters inside 0G Private Compute TEEs, verifying liquidity and quotes.

  2. Check (ENSv2 Identity & on-chain limits)

    AegisExecutionManager queries AegisSubnameRegistry on Sepolia to verify the agent's ENSv2 identity, EAC target contract whitelist, and daily spend limits.

  3. Approve (ENS Owner Gate, only above the threshold)

    Trades at or above the threshold hold onchain until authorization arrives from the registered ENS subname owner.

  4. Execute and record (Uniswap + 0G Storage)

    The trade settles atomically on Uniswap V3 via SwapRouter02 and writes an immutable decision audit record to 0G Storage.

Why AegisNet

Autonomy for agents. Control for owners.

Agents with a raw private key can do anything. AegisNet gives each agent a named identity with hard limits, and brings a verified human in exactly when the stakes are high.

Scoped agent identity

Every agent is registered to its operating address with daily spend limits and whitelists owned by you. Revoke it onchain and the agent is immediately deactivated.

AegisSubnameRegistry

Enforced permissions

A whitelist of target contracts and a daily spend limit live onchain. Requests outside them are rejected before they are queued.

ENS Owner Approval Gate

Anything at or above the agent's threshold pauses until human authorization arrives from the registered ENS subname owner. Each approval works only once with single-use replay protection.

Hackathon Scope & Limitations

AegisNet is currently deployed for testing on Ethereum Sepolia (execution manager & Uniswap V3 swaps) and the 0G Galileo testnet (storage & private compute). Approval thresholds and USD exchange rates are configurable via environment variables for live demonstration.

Three onchain checks in AegisExecutionManager and AegisSubnameRegistry. First, the agent must be registered and active with its contract targets whitelisted. Second, the agent cannot exceed its daily spending limit (tracked and reset daily onchain). Third, any trade at or above the approval threshold pauses onchain until the registered ENS subname owner provides authorization with a single-use nullifier.

If the operator explicitly denies a trade, or if the 15-minute deadline expires without attestation, the pending request is cancelled onchain via cancelExecution (callable by owner, agent, or anyone after deadline). Once cancelled, zero funds move, the transaction cannot be revived, and the single-use request state is sealed.

You do. Agent registration, permission updates, trade submissions, and cancellations are signed directly by your wallet (or your agent's private key) via EIP-1193 or browser providers. The relayer only signs off-chain verification attestations after owner approval passes; it never holds custody of assets or signs wallet transfers.

0G provides two critical primitives: 0G Private Compute runs OpenAI-compatible TEE-attested AI inference to plan optimal trade parameters and verify slippage; 0G Storage permanently stores decentralized JSON audit decision records with Merkle root verification.

Settlement, permission registries, and Uniswap V3 swaps run on Ethereum Sepolia (Chain ID 11155111). Cryptographic audit records and decentralized storage run on the 0G Galileo testnet, with AI planning executed on 0G Private Compute TEE endpoints.

Flash loan arbitrage execution and multi-hop DEX aggregator routing outside Uniswap V3 are out of scope. Mainnet token balances are simulated using realistic market rate quotes for the Sepolia testnet environment.
